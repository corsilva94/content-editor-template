# Content brain map

This is the creator's content brain: everything an AI needs to write and think like them. **Every piece of content starts from this brain. Never invent facts about the creator. If something is missing, ask and offer to save the answer in the right file.**

| When you are doing... | Read first |
|---|---|
| Writing a script or hook | 06-voice, 03-stories, 04-beliefs, 02-audience, templates/script.md |
| Writing captions for an edit | 06-voice (register and banned phrases), the script the video came from |
| Finding ideas | 09-ideas-and-scripts, 02-audience, 04-beliefs, 03-stories |
| Building an offer or call to action | 05-offer, 02-audience, 06-voice |
| Answering in the creator's voice | 06-voice, 01-identity, 04-beliefs |
| Updating the brain | The file's own header; keep one home per fact |

## Folders

- `01-identity/` who the creator is, in their words.
- `02-audience/` who they make content for, and that person's problems.
- `03-stories/` real moments with names, numbers and outcomes.
- `04-beliefs/` opinions and hot takes they will defend on camera.
- `05-offer/` what they sell, who it is for, proof.
- `06-voice/` how they talk, words they use and never use, content boundaries.
- `09-ideas-and-scripts/` ideas, then scripts, dated.
- `templates/` blank script and idea templates.

## Keeping it fresh

When the creator tells you something new, ask whether to save it. If yes, save it in the right file and commit with a clear message such as "Add client story to story bank".

## Before anything goes public

Check the content boundaries in `06-voice/voice.md`.
